---
name: script-style-live
description: Writing rules for the daily "melhores momentos" long video and the Shorts cut from the user's own live (GTA 6 and other games) — his live audio carries the video, the script only picks, orders and frames the moments.
---
- The material is one recording of the user's own live (`user_media`, `keep_audio: true`). Its best moments are in the file's `segments` (long video) — or a single segment (a Short: stay inside it). Every scene is `user_media` with `visual.keep_audio: true`, `narration: ""`, `media_start` inside a segment and `target_seconds` = the piece's length. Never use stock or AI images; never invent what happened — describe only what the segment shows and what he says (read the transcript lines of the segments when given).
- Generated narration (the avatar/voice) is short and only where it frames: the hook at the start, one line before a chapter when the jump needs context, and the closing line. The live itself is the content.
- Long video (platforms youtube, 10–15 min): open on the strongest moment (`hook_type: "best_moment"`) with a one-line hook on screen, then the moments in live order as `blocks` — each block title is a chapter ("A fuga de moto", "O tubarão"), 60–90 s or more when the moment is long. Spoken subscribe ask at ~40% and the end, one line each. Ending: point to the next live ("amanhã tem live às …", time from the channel brief when known) and ask a comment question about the live.
- Short (platforms youtube_shorts, 7–45 s, one moment): hook in the first second as big `on_screen_text` (why keep watching: "ELE NÃO VIU O TUBARÃO 💀"), no dead air at the start — begin right before the action and end on the payoff; nothing still for more than 2–3 s (cut, zoom, reframe); a break around the middle ("mas espera…"); end with a question that splits opinion and a line that loops back to the first second; point to the full live / the melhores momentos video. No spoilers of story missions in the hook or title during launch week — mark "SPOILER" in the caption when the moment is a story beat. Skip nudity; mark violent/sensitive moments.
- Packaging: title ≤ ~40 characters, a question or one word in CAPS, honest to the moment ("Fugi de um TUBARÃO no GTA 6"); thumbnail words ≤ 3 for the long video (Shorts need none).
- Language: the channel's (pt-BR first); adapt, never translate literally, when a sibling channel gets its own version.
