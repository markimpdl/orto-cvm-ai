---
name: script-style-review
description: Writing rules for reviews and unboxings — the presenter reacts to and judges a product from the user's own footage (unboxing recordings with his voice kept where marked), spec slides (PDF) and photos; upbeat, honest delivery.
---
- The material is the user's: `user_media` holds his unboxing/test recordings, photos and, when sent, spec or comparison slides (PDF pages, "Slide N/M de …"). `project.media_mode` is usually "mine": every scene is `user_media` (or a `chart` for specs, prices and benchmarks), plus the presenter's `avatar_full` scenes below; no stock or AI images of the product (they get it wrong). Spec facts come from the slides or the maker's official page (WebSearch) — never invent specs or prices.
- Delivery — different from the lessons: enthusiastic, curious and quick, like showing a friend something new you just got; real reactions ("olha o tamanho disso", "isso aqui me surpreendeu", "okay, this is wild"). Set `voice_style` "upbeat, energetic, smiling, quick but clear" and, in the strongest moments, `delivery` "excited, punchy". Honest: say what disappointed too.
- Opening follows `get_project` → `opening` (off by default on this channel): `hook` false = start straight on the product ("Chegou o … e eu vou abrir com vocês"), no `hook_type`/`hook_variants`/`promise`. `hook` true = a real hook (the verdict teased, the surprise, the price question) + a one-line promise ("no final eu digo se vale o preço"). `bumper` true = set `intro_end` to the scene that ends the opening.
- Recorded parts: a video with `keep_audio: true` (his real unboxing, first power-on, sound test) plays with its own sound — `visual.keep_audio: true`, `narration: ""`, `target_seconds` = the part's length; the generated narration introduces it ("bora abrir:") and comments after it. Unmarked videos play muted under the narration.
- Structure in blocks (YouTube chapters): what it is and the price → what's in the box → design and build → the features that matter, tested (one block each) → pros and cons → verdict: who should buy it, who shouldn't, and whether it's worth the price. For an unboxing-only video: box → first contact → first power-on → first impressions → what you'll test in the full review.
- Use the files in a sensible order (recording order for the unboxing); a long recording may span several consecutive scenes with `media_start`. Name things on screen with `on_screen_text` (model, price, one spec) sparingly.
- Presenter: the podcast desk (`presenter: "podcast"`) stays in the small window over the footage; `avatar_full` for the opening, the verdict and 1–3 strong reactions, ~15–25% of the time.
- Subscribe ask (`cta_points`): short and upbeat, "mid" ~40% and "end". Ending: the verdict in one line (`ending.answer`), a comment question ("você trocaria o seu por esse?"), the next review (`ending.next_video`).
- Packaging: title = product name + the angle (verdict, surprise, price question); `thumbnail_text` = the verdict or reaction in 2–3 words ("VALE MUITO?", "ME ENGANOU", "NÃO COMPRE ANTES"), honest to the video.
- Disclose it when the product was sent by the brand or the video is sponsored (the user says so in the topic or brief): one spoken line near the start.
